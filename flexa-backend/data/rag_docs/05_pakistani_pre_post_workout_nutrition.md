# Pakistani Pre and Post Workout Nutrition Guide

## Core Targets

- Pre-workout: easy carbs + light protein, low heavy fat
- Post-workout: protein + carbs + fluids
- Daily protein target: 1.6-2.2 g/kg body weight

## Pre-Workout Meal Matrix

| Time Before Training | Goal                         | Pakistan-Friendly Options    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 2-3 hours            | Full meal with steady energy | Chicken curry + roti + salad |
| 60-90 min            | Lighter mixed meal           | Oats + milk + banana         |
| 15-30 min            | Quick carbs                  | Dates + banana + water       |

## Post-Workout Meal Matrix

| Time After Training | Goal                           | Pakistan-Friendly Options          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| 0-60 min            | Protein and carbs for recovery | Chicken rice bowl + dahi           |
| 1-2 hours           | Full balanced meal             | Daal + rice + cucumber raita       |
| Snack option        | Fast and simple                | Milk + peanut butter toast + fruit |

## Goal -> Action -> Example

- Goal: Fat loss with gym performance
- Action: Keep pre-workout carbs moderate, keep protein high, control total daily calories
- Example: Pre: banana + black coffee; Post: grilled chicken + mixed vegetables + 1 roti

## Goal -> Action -> Example

- Goal: Muscle gain
- Action: Add 250-350 kcal surplus, include carbs pre and post
- Example: Pre: oats + milk + honey; Post: beef qeema + rice + yogurt

## Problem -> Cause -> Fix

| Problem               | Cause                        | Fix                                           |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Low energy in session | Under-fueled before training | Add 30-60 g carbs 60-90 min pre               |
| Stomach discomfort    | Heavy oily meal before gym   | Shift oily foods away from pre-workout window |
| Poor recovery         | Low protein after training   | Eat 25-40 g protein in first 2 hours          |

## Hydration and Electrolytes

| Situation                   | Action                                                      |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Normal training day         | 2-3 liters water/day minimum                                |
| Hot weather/sweaty sessions | Add pinch of salt + lemon water                             |
| Fasting month training      | Prioritize fluids and electrolytes between iftar and suhoor |

## Budget-Friendly Protein Sources (Pakistan)

- Eggs
- Daal and chana combinations
- Chicken breast or thigh (trimmed)
- Dahi and milk
- Tuna (canned, in water)

## Sample 1-Day Workout Nutrition (Cutting)

- Breakfast: 2 eggs + 1 slice bread + chai (low sugar)
- Lunch: Chicken karahi (controlled oil) + 1 roti + salad
- Pre-workout: Banana + 2 dates
- Post-workout: Daal + rice + dahi
- Dinner: Grilled fish/chicken + vegetables

## Sample 1-Day Workout Nutrition (Bulking)

- Breakfast: Oats + milk + banana + peanuts
- Lunch: Chicken pulao + salad + dahi
- Pre-workout: Peanut butter toast + fruit
- Post-workout: Beef qeema + rice + lassi (low sugar)
- Dinner: Daal + 2 rotis + omelet
