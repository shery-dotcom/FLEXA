# Female-Focused Training and Nutrition Guidance (Beginner to Intermediate)

## Principles

- Train for progression, not punishment
- Adjust intensity by recovery and cycle phase
- Prioritize iron, protein, and hydration consistency

## Cycle-Aware Training (General)

| Phase                          | Common Response                    | Training Adjustment                           |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Early follicular (post-period) | Better energy for many users       | Push heavier compounds                        |
| Late follicular/ovulatory      | High readiness possible            | Performance sessions, PR attempts             |
| Luteal (late cycle)            | Fatigue, bloating, lower tolerance | Reduce volume 10-20%, keep movement quality   |
| Menstruation                   | Variable symptoms                  | Use autoregulation, lower intensity if needed |

## Goal -> Action -> Example

- Goal: Keep consistency despite cycle fluctuations
- Action: Keep exercises fixed, adjust effort and volume only
- Example: Same lower day each week, but in late luteal reduce sets from 4 to 3 and stop at RIR 2-3

## Nutrition Priorities

| Concern                  | Action                                     | Practical Pakistan Example                          |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Low protein intake       | Spread protein across 3-4 meals            | Eggs at breakfast, chicken/daal lunch, yogurt snack |
| Iron support             | Include iron-rich foods + vitamin C source | Beef/chicken liver occasionally, spinach + lemon    |
| Energy dips              | Add pre-workout carbs                      | Banana + dates + water                              |
| Bloating in luteal phase | Reduce very salty processed foods          | Prefer home-cooked meals and steady hydration       |

## Pre/Post Workout (Female-Focused)

| Timing                    | Goal                       | Example                                      |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 60-90 min pre             | Energy + digestion comfort | Oats + milk + banana                         |
| 15-30 min pre (if needed) | Quick carbs                | 2 dates + black coffee                       |
| 0-2 hr post               | Recovery protein + carbs   | Chicken wrap + fruit or daal + rice + yogurt |

## Problem -> Cause -> Fix

| Problem                        | Cause                          | Fix                                 |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Low training output pre-period | Luteal fatigue                 | Lower volume, keep movement quality |
| Dizziness in training          | Low food/hydration             | Pre-workout snack + electrolytes    |
| Slow body recomposition        | Inconsistent protein and steps | Protein target + daily step minimum |

## Strength Training Baseline (3-4 Days)

- Day 1: Lower (squat pattern + hinge)
- Day 2: Upper (push + pull)
- Day 3: Lower (unilateral + posterior chain)
- Day 4: Upper (back focus + accessories)

## Weekly Monitoring

- Sleep hours and quality
- Cycle day and symptoms
- Training RPE trend
- Steps/day and hydration
- Iron status checks if fatigue is persistent

## Refer for Medical Review If

- Very heavy bleeding with fatigue or breathlessness
- Persistent dizziness or fainting episodes
- Missed periods with low energy and under-eating patterns
